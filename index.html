      Good to know

      • Arrival card: everyone needs a Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC), done free at tdac.immigration.go.th in the 3 days before landing, so between 18 and 21 Nov. Use The Berkeley as the address.
      • Visa: Australians get a free 30-day stay on arrival. Passports need 6+ months left.
      • Money: Thai baht (THB). Cards work in hotels and malls; carry cash for markets, tuk-tuks and tips.
      • Power: 220V. Bring adapters: Thai sockets take flat two-pin and round two-pin plugs, not the Australian angled pins.
      • Temples: cover shoulders and knees, shoes off inside. The Grand Palace is strict.
      • Weather: late Nov is cool season. Bangkok and Hua Hin around 31–33°C; Chiang Mai mornings can drop to 16–18°C and Doi Inthanon's summit is colder.
      • Getting around: Grab (like Uber) works in all three cities and has larger cars. Agree a price before getting into a tuk-tuk.
      • Time: Thailand is 3 hours behind Brisbane and 4 hours behind Sydney (daylight saving).
      • 5 Dec is a national public holiday (King Bhumibol's birthday, Father's Day). Some offices close; airports run as normal.
